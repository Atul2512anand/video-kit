# Brag Plan: BuildForge Platform

## What is this app?
BuildForge is becoming a community-led platform — not an agency: a talent
marketplace (Talent), learning community (Connect), founder meetups (Circle),
and verified Talent Passport, introduced one piece at a time.

## The angle
"NOT AN AGENCY ANYMORE." The video dramatizes the strategic shift itself: from
selling work the team delivers, to a platform where founders and emerging
talent find each other. Every number on screen is the doc's own illustration
(₹5,000 → ₹500, 12–15 seats × ₹2,500).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"NOT AN AGENCY." slams in, crosses out, becomes "A PLATFORM." Then the
positioning line: "Where Emerging Talent Meets Opportunity."

## Key moments (the middle)
- 3 pillar cards arriving one by one: TALENT (marketplace · 10% commission),
  CONNECT (mentorship · masterclasses), CIRCLE (12–15 founders · ₹2,500).
- Money demo counting up: ₹5,000 project → ₹500 fee → ₹4,500 to freelancer.
- Workflow strip: brief → match → deliver → passport (verified badge).
- Circle ticket card: 12–15 seats, one practical topic, pilot economics.

## Outro / punchline
"START WITH ONE PILOT PROJECT." + buildforge.site + ceo@buildforge.site.

## User flow worth showing
The doc's 8-step marketplace workflow, compressed to 4 beats: brief → match →
deliver → passport. Shown as a moving strip, not a list.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium platform launch film
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds than chaotic — but NO dead air:
  every second carries a reveal, a count-up, or a card arrival. The failure of
  the last cut (static 6s hold, untimed SFX) is designed out here.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with purpose — EVERY sound lands on a visual event
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady, 109.96 BPM)
- Music treatment: start 0s, 0.35 bed, dip under the money count, fade 18-20s,
  final bell rings over fade
- Music cue guidance: cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 preset; lock the
  "A PLATFORM." reveal (~2.5s) and the passport stamp (~13s) within ±0.15s of
  strong cues; snap pillar cards + workflow steps to every-other-beat grid
  (~1.1s spacing, readable); ignore cues that hurt legibility
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; RMS breathes the purple arc glow + card
  presence only. No waveforms.
- SFX posture: moderate, strictly motion-matched — a sound for every arrival,
  silence everywhere else
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Hook cross-out "AGENCY → PLATFORM" — snap + announcement hit
  - 3 pillar cards — card-place sounds on beats 1, 2, 3 of the sequence
  - Money count-up ticks — tick accents on each number landing
  - Passport stamp — deep bell, VERIFIED
  - Ticket stub slide — card-slide
  - Outro CTA — impactBell_heavy_000, let ring
- Restraint rule: no sound without a simultaneous visual; no stacked hits

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
On screen: off-white, "NOT AN AGENCY." giant, diagonal cross-out stroke draws,
"AGENCY" replaced by "A PLATFORM." in purple. Then positioning line small.
Sequential/interaction: cross-out draws (0.4s), word swap, hold.
Audio intent: snap + bed establishes.
Audio-coupled idea: interface snap at cross-out, soft bell at "PLATFORM."
Music: vol-12 bed from 0s
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Pillars — 5s (3-8s)
On screen: "ONE PLATFORM. FOUR PARTS." + 3 cards arriving one by one: TALENT
(marketplace · 10% commission) / CONNECT (mentorship · masterclasses) /
CIRCLE (12–15 founders · ₹2,500). (Passport saved for Scene 3 as the payoff.)
Sequential/interaction: yes — cards arrive on every-other-beat with card sounds.
Audio intent: building confidence, rhythmic.
Audio-coupled idea: card-place accents on each arrival (3 hits max).
Music: same bed
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Proof — 8s (8-16s)
On screen: dark #232326. Money demo: "₹5,000" counts up → "− ₹500 fee" →
"= ₹4,500 to freelancer" (each landing with a tick). Then workflow strip:
brief → match → deliver → PASSPORT badge stamps VERIFIED with bell. Then Circle
ticket stub slides in: "CIRCLE PILOT · 12–15 seats · ₹2,500".
Sequential/interaction: yes — numbers then steps then stamp then stub, each
≈1.1s apart, each with its sound.
Audio intent: numbers tick, stamp lands heavy, stub slides light.
Audio-coupled idea: counter ticks, bell on VERIFIED, card-slide on stub.
Music: bed dips slightly under count, recovers
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s (16-20s)
On screen: "START WITH ONE PILOT PROJECT." + buildforge.site +
ceo@buildforge.site. Purple dot pulse. Elements keep micro-motion (no frozen
frame): dot breathes, CTA holds full 4s.
Sequential/interaction: none — settled holds with living accents.
Audio intent: bed fades 18-20s, final bell rings over fade.
Audio-coupled idea: impactBell_heavy_000 at CTA landing, let ring.
Music: fade out 18-20s
Transition mood: end (0.5s tail, poster-safe)

**Music mood for this video:** warm steady corporate bed
**Audio summary:** Bed at 0.35 with one dip, a cued sound on every arrival and
landing, bells on the two payoffs (PLATFORM reveal, VERIFIED stamp, outro) —
the opposite of the last cut: nothing moves silently, nothing sounds empty.
