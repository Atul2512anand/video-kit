# SOURCE — BuildForge Platform Revamp & Business Plan (v1.0, September 2026)

A practical blueprint for a talent marketplace, founder community, mentorship
network, and curated meetups. Working strategy document — decision-making
blueprint, not validated claims.

Key facts used in the video (all verbatim or lightly trimmed from this doc):

- Working positioning: **Where Emerging Talent Meets Opportunity.**
- Strategic shift: away from traditional agency; enable users to find each
  other, agree terms, complete work, build a record.
- Four pillars:
  - BuildForge Talent — marketplace for freelance projects — commission on
    completed transactions (illustrative 10%).
  - BuildForge Connect — community, webinars, mentorship — masterclasses,
    booking commission, sponsorships.
  - BuildForge Circle — small curated founder meetups — tickets, sponsorships.
  - Talent Passport — verified record of projects and skills.
- Illustrative transaction: ₹5,000 project → ₹500 fee (10%) → ₹4,500 freelancer.
- Circle pilot: 12–15 attendees, ~₹2,500 ticket.
- 8-step marketplace workflow: brief → review → apply/invite → agree terms →
  work → review → pay → feedback + passport.
- Launch messaging (founders): "Need a defined project done?"
- Launch messaging (students): "Turn your skills into real project experience."
- 90-day plan: narrow pilot → concierge → repeatability → monetization test.
- Closing principle: make opportunity easier to access.
